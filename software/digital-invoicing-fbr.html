Digital Invoicing (FBR)

FBR digital invoices with HS codes and provincial tax handled. For sales-tax registered businesses that must issue FBR digital invoices.

Freelive demo

Made for Pakistan's FBR digital invoicing requirement. HS codes, units of measure and the correct provincial sales tax are applied automatically, and each invoice is validated before it goes out.

How it works

  1. Set up your products with their HS codes once.
  2. Create the invoice. Tax type and rates are filled in for the buyer's province.
  3. The invoice is validated and submitted.
  4. Keep the digital record for reporting.

What's included

  • HS code syncCodes applied automatically, so you aren't typing them.
  • Units of measureCorrect UoM for each product.
  • Provincial sales taxPicks the right tax type for each province.
  • ValidationCatches mistakes before submission.
