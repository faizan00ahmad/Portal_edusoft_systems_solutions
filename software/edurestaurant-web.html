EduRestaurant Manager runs the whole restaurant from one system. Orders are taken at the counter, at the table or on the waiter's phone, go straight to the kitchen, and the bill is ready when the guest asks for it.

Behind the counter it keeps the menu, raw material stock, purchases, staff and accounts, so the owner sees sales, costs and profit for every day without adding anything up by hand.

How it works

  1. Set up the menu, deals, tables and kitchen sections once.
  2. Take a dine-in, takeaway or delivery order. The kitchen gets its order ticket (KOT) instantly.
  3. Print the bill with discounts and taxes, and take cash, card or credit.
  4. Stock is reduced by recipe, and the day closes with sales, cash and profit reports.

What's included

  • Point of saleFast billing for dine-in, takeaway and delivery.
  • Tables & waitersTable status, orders per waiter and bill splitting.
  • Kitchen order ticketsOrders printed or shown in the right kitchen section.
  • Menu & dealsItems, sizes, add-ons, deals and price changes.
  • Recipe stockRaw material used per dish, so stock stays right.
  • Purchases & suppliersPurchase orders, stock in and supplier balances.
  • AccountsCash book, expenses, ledgers and day closing.
  • Sales reportsItem-wise, waiter-wise, hour-wise and daily profit.

Modules in detail

Front of house

  • Bill split, merge and transfer between tables

Kitchen

  • Kitchen order tickets by section
  • Order status from kitchen to table

Inventory

Finance

Questions we get

Can waiters take orders on a phone?

Yes. The Restaurant Manager Android app sends orders from the table straight to the kitchen.

Does it work for more than one branch?

Yes. Each branch has its own sales and stock, and the owner sees all of them.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser; open the demo with the login shown.
