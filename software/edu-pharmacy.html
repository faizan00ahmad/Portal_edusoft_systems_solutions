Edu Pharmacy Manager

Pharmacy ERP: counter billing, batch and expiry, purchases and accounts. For medical stores, pharmacies, pharmacy chains and hospital pharmacies.

Edu Pharmacy Manager runs the pharmacy from the counter to the accounts. Every medicine is tracked by batch and expiry, so the right stock is sold first and nothing expires on the shelf unnoticed.

Purchases, supplier returns, stock between branches and the day's cash are all in one system, with reports the owner can check at any time.

How it works

  1. Add medicines with generic name, pack size, batch and expiry.
  2. Receive purchases from distributors; stock comes in by batch.
  3. Bill at the counter with barcode or name search; the oldest batch goes out first.
  4. Get expiry alerts, reorder lists and the day's sales and profit.

What's included

  • Counter billingFast sales by barcode, brand or generic name.
  • Batch & expiryStock by batch, expiry alerts and near-expiry lists.
  • Purchases & returnsPurchase invoices, bonuses and returns to suppliers.
  • Stock levelsMinimum levels and automatic reorder lists.
  • Generic substitutesFind alternatives by generic name.
  • Branches & warehouseStock transfers between branches.
  • AccountsSupplier ledgers, cash book and expenses.
  • ReportsSales, profit, stock value and expiry reports.
