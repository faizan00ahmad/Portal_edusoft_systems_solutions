Edu Enterprise

Enterprise ERP: manufacturing, inventory, sales, purchase, finance and HR. For manufacturers and growing companies with several departments or branches.

Edu Enterprise is a full ERP for companies that make and sell. Production, raw material and finished goods stock, purchasing, sales, finance and HR run in one system, so every department works from the same numbers.

Management sees costs, stock, receivables and profit across the whole company, branch by branch and department by department.

How it works

  1. Set up products, bills of material, warehouses, departments and the chart of accounts.
  2. Purchase raw material; it comes into the store against the purchase order.
  3. Issue material to production, record output, and move finished goods into stock.
  4. Sell and dispatch; accounts, costing and reports update across the company.

What's included

  • ManufacturingBills of material, production orders and costing.
  • InventoryRaw material, work in progress and finished goods.
  • PurchasingRequisitions, purchase orders, receiving and returns.
  • Sales & dispatchQuotations, sales orders, invoices and deliveries.
  • FinanceGeneral ledger, banks, payables and receivables.
  • HR & payrollEmployees, attendance, leave and salaries.
  • BranchesSeveral companies, branches and warehouses.
  • Management reportsCost, stock, sales and profit dashboards.
