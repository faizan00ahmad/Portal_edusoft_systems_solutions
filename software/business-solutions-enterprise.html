Accounts, HR & ERP

Business Solutions Enterprise

ERP for companies with several branches or businesses. For distributors, manufacturers and groups of companies.

Our largest system. It handles several companies and branches under one login, with accounts, stock, purchasing, sales, payroll and banking connected.

Distributors use it for area-wise sales, targets and trade schemes. Manufacturers use it for batch-wise stock and campaigns.

What's included

  • Multiple companies & branchesSeparate books per unit, consolidated where you need it.
  • AccountsDouble entry with your own chart of accounts and one-click year closing.
  • StockBatch-wise stock, barcodes, several stock holders and stock revaluation.
  • Purchasing & salesTax setup, sales orders, trade schemes, areas and targets.
  • PayrollBiometric attendance, salaries, leave and loans.
  • BankingBank accounts, reconciliation, receipts and payments.
  • User rightsControl exactly which screens each user can open.
  • MIS reportsBalance sheet, stock ageing, working capital and comparisons.

Questions we get

How long does setup take?

It depends on the number of branches and how much old data you have. We'll give you a plan after the first meeting.

Does it work with our biometric machines?

It supports biometric attendance import. Tell us your machine model and we'll confirm.
