Business Solution for retail stores: billing, stock in, stock out and accounts. For retail shops, marts, superstores and stores with one or more counters.

EduBS RT (Business Solution, Retail) is built for retail stores. Counter billing, purchases, stock in and stock out, customer and supplier accounts and financial reports all run in one system.

Every sale reduces stock and posts to accounts automatically, so the owner always knows what is on the shelf, what is owed, and what the store earned today.

How it works

  1. Add items with barcode, category, purchase and sale price.
  2. Record purchases from suppliers. Stock comes in and the supplier's balance updates.
  3. Sell at the counter with a barcode scanner. Stock goes out and the sale posts to accounts.
  4. Check stock, receivables, payables and profit from the reports.

What's included

  • Counter billing (POS)Barcode billing on one or more counters.
  • PurchasesPurchase orders, purchase invoices and returns.
  • Stock in / stock outEvery movement recorded with date and reason.
  • Stock levelsCurrent stock, minimum levels and reorder lists.
  • Customers & creditCredit sales, receipts and customer ledgers.
  • SuppliersPayables, payments and supplier ledgers.
  • Cash & bankCash book, bank accounts and expenses.
  • Financial reportsDaily sales, profit and loss, balance sheet.
